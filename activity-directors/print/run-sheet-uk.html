Run a Quiz Hour — United Kingdom

What you need

  • A TV or projector with internet — any smart TV, streaming stick, or laptop that plays YouTube.
  • Scorecards & pencils — optional. One card per player, or one per table. Or just shout your answers — that works just as well.
  • Tables of up to 6 — team play makes the room lively.
  • About 30–45 minutes: one episode, played with pauses.

Troubleshooting

  • Hard to hear? Set the volume first, and turn subtitles ON (the "CC" button) — every question is read aloud and shown in large type.
  • Answer comes too fast? Freeze on the 7-second timer with the pause button as long as the table needs.
  • Video stutters? Pause 30 seconds to let it load, or lower quality (gear icon → Quality → 720p).
  • Lost your place? Chapters are listed under the video — tap one to jump to a round.

The hour, step by step

  1. Open the series. Go to youtube.com/@60PlusQuizClub, open the Britain series and pick an episode from the menu below.
  2. Hand out scorecards (or don't — fingers and loud voices keep score fine).
  3. Pause after each question. The video gives 7 seconds to think. Pause there, let everyone shout, debate, and settle — then press play for the reveal.
  4. Mark them pub-quiz style. Swap sheets with another table or player to mark them, then swap back — just like a proper pub quiz.
  5. Read the rank table at the end — from Warming Up to Quiz Champion — and announce it out loud.
  6. Same time next week. New Britain episodes land Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, so Quiz Hour holds a fixed slot on your calendar.
