Each episode is a complete activity session: 50 questions in four rounds — Easy, Medium, Hard, and Expert — about the America your residents grew up in: state capitals, what a gallon of gas cost in 1960, the TV shows, the cars, the diners, and the moments everybody remembers. How to run it with a group

  1. Pick the episode from the menu above and open it on the TV or projector. Any smart TV, streaming stick, or laptop with YouTube works.
  2. Hand out the scorecards (one per player, or one per table for team play) and pencils.
  3. Pause after each question. The video gives 7 seconds to think — pause it there and let everyone shout out, debate, and settle on an answer before you press play for the reveal. This is where the fun is; take your time.
  4. Let the table talk. Teams of up to six around one table work beautifully — talking each answer through together is half the activity.
  5. Finish with the score table. The episode ends with the rankings. Read out who made Quiz Champion, celebrate the Warming Up crowd, and take requests for next time — the holiday and decade episodes are group favorites.
  6. Make it a fixture. New episodes land every Monday, Wednesday and Friday, so "Quiz Club" can sit in the same slot on your activity calendar week after week.
